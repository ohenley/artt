Source: **patrickhenrypdx**, who states they measured their own XA for another restorer. [Original measurement post](https://www.reddit.com/r/turntables/comments/1t5vqzh/ar_xa_tone_arm_counter_weight_tone_arm_rest/). Checked 2026-11-04. Values below preserve the reported precision; no manufacturing tolerances were supplied.

## Counterweight

| Feature | Reported value |
| --- | --- |
| Outside diameter | 28 mm |
| Length along bore | 22 mm |
| Bore diameter | 9.5 mm |
| Mass, including set screw | 98.8 g |
| Material | Brass |

This is a plausible lathe-work candidate, **our assessment**. A finished drawing still needs the bore fit against the actual arm stub, whether the bore is centered, locking-hole thread and position, screw tip/length, edge details, and acceptable mass/adjustment range. A nominal 9.5 mm owner measurement is not a bore tolerance.

The contributor's material identification and measurements have not been independently checked. Keep this as a measurement record until the original part can be inspected and a replacement tested. Second measurements are tracked in the [confirmation list](../TO-CONFIRM.md#counterweight-and-rest). A [preliminary drawing and STEP file](../cad/README.md) are built from these values, with the missing locking-hole details shown as placeholders.
