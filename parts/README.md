Checked **2026-11-04**. Listings establish what a maker advertises, not independent fit testing. Confirm your motor, voltage/frequency, original versus replacement arm, and relevant dimensions. Prices and stock are deliberately omitted.

Looking to make a part? Start with the [part-by-part reproduction index](reproduction.md). The first additional measurement record covers an [XA counterweight and tonearm-rest height](xa-counterweight.md).

For motors, capacitors, bearings, and generic fasteners, first establish the actual specification on the model page. A verified modern motor/capacitor combination and a complete hardware shopping list have **not** yet been established.

Original-style XA/XB headshell screws and spacers now have a [dimensioned community reference](../docs/headshells.md#mounting-screws-and-spacers), alongside the original AR mounting sheet. Keep these separate from a reproduction shell's supplied screw specification.
