# Motor and pulley

[Find your part](README.md) · [Part index](reproduction.md) · [Belt and pulley mod](../models/xa/mods/belt-and-pulley.md) · [Things to confirm](../TO-CONFIRM.md#motor-electrical-and-overall-size)

## What it is

The synchronous motor under the top plate and the stepped pulley on its shaft; the belt runs from the pulley to the inner platter, and moving the belt between the two steps selects 33⅓ or 45 rpm. A synchronous motor's speed follows the mains frequency, so a 50 Hz table needs its own pulley. Photo wanted: a Haydon and a Philips motor side by side, pulleys fitted.

## Drawings

None. Missing for a pulley drawing: the step diameters, the bore for each motor, and the belt section, measured on an original of each kind. The motor is a part to buy, not to make: no current substitute with its shaft, mounting, pulley and capacitor is recorded (BP1, EL1).

## If you make or change it

**Keep:**

- **The shaft diameter and the pulley bore:** ⅛ in Haydon, ¹⁄₁₆ in Philips (Factory, service manual p. 1).
- **The pulley diameters for your mains:** 50 Hz and 60 Hz pulleys differ, and the belt step sets 33⅓ or 45 rpm.
- **The mounting:** the XB motor sits on bracket 500056 with two #4-48 × ⅛ in screws (Factory, drawing 119); the XA motor is held by two screws whose size is not stated.
- **The capacitor** that suits the motor: 0.1 µF at 120 V and 0.39 µF at 220 V on the XA, 600 VDC rated (Factory, service manual p. 3); the XB values are Unconfirmed, EL4.

**Free:** nothing on the pulley beyond its finish. A motor is a bought part.

**What else is affected:**

- **Speed:** check it with the [strobe disc](strobe-disc.md) after any change. The manual's fix for a slow table is a shim under the motor mounting screw nearest the platter, and for a fast one the opposite (Factory, service manual p. 1).
- **The belt:** see the [belt and pulley mod](../models/xa/mods/belt-and-pulley.md); a belt that is too tight pulls the suspension toward the motor (Maker claim, [Vinyl Nirvana belt](../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/products/replacement-ar-turntable-belt.md)).
- **The capacitor and the switch:** the [motor capacitor mod](../models/xa/mods/motor-capacitor.md).
- **Noise:** the [motor grommets mod](../models/xa/mods/motor-grommets.md).

## Check after fitting

1. Belt on the upper step, outer platter on. Switch on: the platter starts and runs the right way.
2. Strobe disc under a mains lamp: the 33⅓ pattern stands still, or drifts no more than 21 lines in a minute.
3. Move the belt to the lower step and check 45 rpm the same way.
4. Listen at the inner groove for hum and rumble with the volume up and the stylus on a silent groove.
5. Record the motor label, the pulley, and the capacitor markings on your table's page.
