## What it is

The wooden box that carries the metal top plate, with the motor, switch and capacitors inside it and a bottom panel underneath. Photo wanted: an original XA base with the top plate off.

## If you make or change it

**Keep:**

- **The top plate seat:** the plate must sit flat on its ledge at the original height, or the arm, the platter and the cover no longer line up (PL15, PL16, PL62).
- **Clearance for the subchassis swing, the motor, and the suspension studs:** the T-bar moves on its springs and must touch nothing (PL60, PL61); the replacement suspension kit uses 2 in studs (Maker claim, [Vinyl Nirvana kit](../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/products/upgrade-spring-kit-xa-xb-xb-77.md)), and one report of other studs being too long for a stock plinth is logged as SK1.
- **The cable exit** (PL63), so the arm wires and the output cable reach the terminal strip without pulling on the arm.
- **On the XB:** at most 2.36 in of amplifier cable and arm wire inside the cabinet, and the motor capacitor at least ⅛ in from the top plate (Factory, [ECO 1315, sheet 2](../docs/drawings/xb-drawing-116.png)).

**Free:** the wood, the finish, the outside profile, the feet, and new cutouts in the rear wall as long as they are clear of the swing.

**What else is affected:**

- **The dust cover:** a new base must take the old cover, or a [new cover](../models/xa/mods/dust-cover.md). A hinged XB cover needs the [hinge template](hinge-template.md).
- **The suspension:** stud length against plinth depth, SK1, in the [suspension kit mod](../models/xa/mods/suspension-kit.md).
- **The wiring:** the [RCA jacks](../models/xa/mods/rca-jacks.md), [IEC inlet](../models/xa/mods/iec-inlet.md) and [tonearm rewire](../models/xa/mods/tonearm-rewire.md) mods, bundled in the [plinth rebuild](../models/xa/mods/plinth-rebuild.md).

## Check after fitting

1. The top plate sits flat on the ledge with no rocking, and its screw holes line up.
2. Push the platter sideways and let go: the subchassis swings free and touches nothing inside.
3. The motor runs and nothing inside touches the walls or the bottom panel.
4. The cover closes with the arm in its rest.
5. Unplugged: continuity from the mains earth to the top plate if you bonded it, and no continuity from line or neutral to the plate.
