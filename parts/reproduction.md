# Making replacement parts

[Home](../README.md) · [Find your part](README.md) · [CAD](../cad/README.md) · [Original drawings](../docs/README.md)

Part index for the first-family tables: XA, XB, and AR77XB. No second-family part has a row yet: parts for The AR Turntable, EB-101, ES-1 and ETL-1 are on their [model pages](../models/README.md#second-family-1983-onward). A row is a worklist entry, not a drawing. Each row becomes a part page over time, with the same layout on every page: versions, what fits what, drawings, what to keep when making one. Pages so far: [counterweight](counterweight.md), [headshell](headshell.md), [cartridge screws, bushings and shims](cartridge-hardware.md), [overhang template](overhang-template.md), [base or plinth](plinth.md), [motor and pulley](motor-and-pulley.md), [tonearm rest](tonearm-rest.md), [strobe disc](strobe-disc.md), [platter height gauge](platter-height-gauge.md), [cueing height gauge](cueing-height-gauge.md), [hinge drilling template](hinge-template.md).

**Evidence:** *dimensioned* = some dimensions documented; *assembly only* = illustrated or identified; *missing* = no usable source.

## Body and cover

| Part | Possible making method | Evidence | Needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wooden base / plinth | Woodworking or CNC routing | Assembly only; [plinth page](plinth.md) | Wall sections, rebates, fixing positions, finish |
| Metal top plate | Cut and drill sheet; form | Assembly only; [XB drawing 119](../docs/drawings/xb-drawing-119.png) | Hole pattern, thickness, material, edges |
| Bottom panel | Cut sheet and drill | Assembly only, XB | Outline, thickness, attachment, cable exit |
| Dust cover | Cut, bend, or bond acrylic | Dimensioned in part; [XB hinge detail](../models/xb/README.md#cover-dimensions-and-clearance) | Shape, thickness, lip, corners, clearances by version |
| Hinges and brackets | Form sheet or machine | Dimensioned in part; [XB drawing 114](../docs/drawings/xb-drawing-114.png), [hinge drilling template](hinge-template.md). Later XB revisions delete hinges | Hinge mechanism, pin fits |
| Feet / pads | Cut or mold | Felt feet on the XB BOM | Footprint, thickness, material |
| Badges / bezels | Engrave, print, or cut | Assembly only | Geometry, artwork, attachment |
