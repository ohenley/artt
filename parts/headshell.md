## What it is

The plug-in holder at the end of the arm that the cartridge bolts into; its round neck carries four contact pins into the arm's socket and a knurled nut holds it (Factory, [XA service manual, p. 4](../docs/originals/xa-service-manual.pdf), items 20 and 21C). Photo wanted: an original XA shell, neck end and underside, beside a rule.

## If you make or change it

**Keep:**

- **The neck and its pins.** The neck diameter, length, thread for the knurled nut, the key, and the four-pin layout must match the arm's socket (HD10 to HD15, HD19, HD20). Nothing else makes the shell fit.
- **The height of the mounting plane against the arm tube axis** (HD12). It sets the cartridge's angle to the record. The XB test is that the shell's mounting bosses are parallel to the turntable surface within ±1° (Factory, drawing 119, note 10).
- **½ in stud spacing.** The XB1 manual gives the cartridge mounting as ½ in EIA centres (Factory, [XB1 manual, p. 2](../docs/originals/xb1-owners.pdf)). The Grado's own spacing is GR11.
- **The inside width,** at least 0.805 in (20.45 mm), because the shim block lies across it (derived from the [community drawing](../docs/originals/ar-headshell-hardware-rev-1.pdf), HD27).
- **The mass.** 7 g was the factory design value for the acrylic shell (Factory, Villchur 1962, p. 22). Villchur's point: a lighter shell needs a smaller counterweight and lowers the arm's inertia. Each drawing prints the model's mass against 7 g.
- **The thread, on an original-pattern shell:** #3-48, major diameter 0.099 in (2.51 mm), pitch 0.529 mm (Community drawing checked on 14 XA and XB shells; thread table, [Wikipedia, Unified Thread Standard](../docs/archive/wikipedia/unified-thread-standard.md)).

**Free:** the outside shape, the colour, the finger lift's shape as long as it is strong, and top-mount slots in place of studs for a cartridge that takes them.

**What else is affected:**

- **The [counterweight](counterweight.md):** a heavier shell needs more counterweight travel or a heavier weight; AR's extra section exists for that.
- **The overhang:** every new shell or cartridge is followed by the [overhang template](overhang-template.md); the XA has an overhang set screw on the arm for it.
- **The [cartridge screws, bushings and shims](cartridge-hardware.md):** a shell with different studs takes different screws. Pat's Audio uses M2.5.
- **Arm resonance:** mass at the end of the arm with the cartridge's compliance sets it; GR12.

## Check after fitting

1. Plug the shell in and tighten the nut by hand. Wiggle it: no play.
2. Ohmmeter from each cartridge clip to the matching terminal under the table: four connections, no shorts.
3. Balance the arm and set the stylus force. The counterweight must reach it.
4. Check the overhang with the template. Adjust and recheck.
5. Lay a small square on the record against the shell's side: the mounting bosses parallel within ±1° (XB note 10).
6. Play the inner grooves with the motor running: no hum, no skipping, and the shell clears the record.
