## If you make or change it

**Keep:** the installed height, so the arm drops into the yoke level and the stylus clears the record and the mat; the yoke width, so the arm tube snaps in without scratching; the stem thread, #5-40 on the XB.

**Free:** the yoke's outside shape, colour and material, as long as the arm tube is not marked by it.

**What else is affected:** the [counterweight](counterweight.md), which must clear the rest at the back of the arm's swing (CW16); the cover, which must close over the arm in its rest.

## Check after fitting

1. Lift the arm, swing it to the rest, and let it drop in. It must snap in and stay.
2. With the cover closed, nothing touches the arm or the rest.
3. Lift the arm out of the rest and back ten times. No marks on the arm tube.
