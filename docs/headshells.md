## Mounting screws and spacers

[Open the dimensioned drawing](originals/ar-headshell-hardware-rev-1.pdf). For why there are so many pieces and where they go, see [What it is and where it goes](what-goes-where.md).

This is a **community drawing**, signed **RDW, December 7, 2023**, shared by **ronaldw441**. It is not an AR factory manufacturing drawing. The poster describes checking the #3-48 thread against 14 original XA/XB shells. [Source discussion, posts 2 and 4](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/acoustic-research-ar77xb-search-for-new-headshell-bolts.407382/)

| Part | Drawing dimensions, inches |
| --- | --- |
| Aluminum cartridge screws | #3-48; lengths under head: 0.183, 0.356, 0.462, 0.612; two of each |
| Brass set screw | #4-40 × 0.125; one |
| Soft plastic bushings | ID 0.100, OD 0.180; heights 0.050, 0.100, 0.150; two of each |
| Cardboard shims | 0.805 × 0.230; thicknesses 0.045, 0.080, 0.125 |

These dimensions describe the pictured original-style hardware set, not the thread specification of every reproduction. The drawing explicitly names **XA and XB**; sharing it in an AR77XB thread does not establish every AR77XB variant.

## Original AR mounting illustration

[Open AR's mounting sheet, L-1591](originals/ar-cartridge-mount.jpg).

The sheet includes a headshell/cartridge sketch, mounting-stud and shim-block section, original wire-color information, and a cartridge-specific screw/spacer selection table. It describes the shell as "a light plastic" with threaded mounting studs. It explains the mounting arrangement, but gives no complete shell-body dimensions. [Scan source, post 2](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/acoustic-research-ar77xb-search-for-new-headshell-bolts.407382/)

## Body and connector drawing search

The [factory drawing archive](https://audioappraisal.com/acoustic-research-drawings-archive/) includes the XB final assembly. It identifies the cartridge-shell assembly as **404003**, but the inspected assembly sheet does not provide the dimensions needed to manufacture that part. No separate headshell entry was found in the archive index.

A [3D-printed AR-XA replacement by redcedarprints](https://www.ebay.com/itm/144937941911) is a possible design-holder lead; no downloadable CAD/STL or reproduction license was verified. The listing returned HTTP 403 when rechecked on 2026-11-04, so neither its contents nor its availability are confirmed.

A [PMMA top-mount concept](../cad/README.md#the-pmma-headshell) follows the layout in the XA service manual's exploded view. Its body and connector dimensions are placeholders, not evidence.

For a reproducible body drawing, the remaining evidence needs to establish the neck profile and locking geometry, pin positions and travel, cartridge mounting-hole positions, body dimensions, and material/mass. None is inferred by scaling the general assembly illustration.
