## XB factory drawings

From [Audio Appraisal's archive](https://audioappraisal.com/acoustic-research-drawings-archive/). All eight TIFFs were downloaded and visually inspected. **112–119 are archive index numbers, not AR part or drawing numbers.** Assembly sheets identify AR drawing 100012; revisions differ.

For easier reading, [open the combined 8-page PDF](xb-factory-drawings.pdf). Pages follow archive entries 112–119 in order, with entry 113 rotated upright. The original TIFFs remain unchanged below.

| Archive entry | Local original | Content / locator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 112 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-112.tif) | Earlier XB assembly; superseded stamp. Historical comparison. |
| 113 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-113.tif) | Revision S, sheet 1 of 2. Assembly, wiring detail, and BOM; scan is upside down as supplied. |
| 114 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-114.tif) | Revision S, sheet 2. Hinge locating and switch detail. |
| 115 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-115.tif) | ECO 1315, sheet 1 of 4. January 28, 1977: deletes hinge assemblies and associated hardware. |
| 116 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-116.tif) | ECO 1315, sheet 2. Cable routing and capacitor clearance. |
| 117 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-117.tif) | ECO 1315, sheet 3. Capacitor mounting straps and tubing. |
| 118 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-118.tif) | ECO 1315, sheet 4. Removal of XB1-specific rework parts. |
| 119 | [TIFF](originals/xb-drawing-119.tif) | Revision T final assembly. BOM at right, adjustment notes below, audio wiring detail at upper right. Superseded stamp is visible. |

Extracted hardware and electrical facts are on the [XB page](../models/xb/README.md). ECO sheets 2–4 are dated October 21, 1976, and sheet 1 January 28, 1977. All four sheets also carry a March 30, 1977 date stamp, which the archive index lists as the file date. Those dates should not be silently treated as table manufacture dates or serial cutoffs.

## Preservation

[manifest.tsv](manifest.tsv) records each local file's origin, retrieval date, SHA-256, and any page selection. TIFFs, the XB1 PDF, the XA service manual, and the two headshell files are unmodified downloads; paper PDFs are explicitly identified as page selections. The combined drawing PDF is a reading copy made from the TIFFs. Full magazine downloads are kept outside the repository to keep it small.

Audio Appraisal states that AR released the drawings into the public domain in 1990; that statement has not been independently established here. Third-party scans and articles retain their own rights and attribution. The repository's [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](../LICENSE) covers work written for it, not these files.
