# The first hour with a used AR turntable

[Home](../README.md) · [Document index](README.md) · [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) · [Owner registry](registry.md)

You have just brought one home. This page covers what to check before it goes on the mains, how to carry it, and what the factory put in the box, so you can see what is missing.

Most of it comes from first-family papers: the XA owner's manual, the XA service manual and the XB1 manual. For The AR Turntable, EB-101, ES-1 and ETL-1 no owner's manual is held; their rows say Unconfirmed.

## Before you plug it in

**Do not turn it upside down.** AR says to brace the table on its side and never invert it (Factory, [XA service manual, p. 2](originals/xa-service-manual.pdf)). Oil runs out of the bearings, and the platter and arm hang on springs.

**Check the mains voltage and frequency first.** A 120 V XA and a 220 V XA have different capacitors and wiring (Factory, XA service manual, p. 3). The XB1 is a 220 to 240 V table with a 3 A fuse (Factory, [XB1 manual, p. 16](transcripts/xb1-owners-manual.md)). The motor is synchronous, so a 60 Hz table runs slow on 50 Hz mains unless the larger 50 cycle pulley is fitted (Factory, XA service manual, p. 1). A wrong-voltage table can be damaged the moment it is switched on (our assessment).

1. Read the label under the base. Write down the serial number, the voltage and the frequency. Where to find it: [owner registry](registry.md#where-the-serial-number-is). Expected: a voltage that matches your mains. If it does not match, stop here.
2. Lift off the mat and the outer platter. Hold the outer platter by its rim and lift it straight up. Try not to hit the pulley (Factory, [XA owner's manual, p. 11](transcripts/xa-owners-manual.md)).
3. XA only: count the motors you can see. Two motors means a serial up to XA66866 (Factory, XA service manual, p. 1). Its parts have not been available for years (same page).
4. Look at the belt. It should run round the inner platter and the upper, smaller pulley step for 33⅓ rpm, with no twists (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 4). A shiny, sticky or cracked belt needs cleaning or replacing: [troubleshooting, slow start](troubleshooting.md#slow-start-or-wrong-speed).
5. XB pattern: look for a wing-nut transit bolt, perhaps with a red CAUTION tag, holding the suspension to the top plate, and for foam shims under the inner platter. Remove both (Factory, XB1 manual, pp. 2–3). Keep the bolt for moving the table. On the XB drawings the shipping bolt is item 8, part 501011, with a nylon washer, item 7 (Factory, [What it is and where it goes](what-goes-where.md)). Where it sits on an XB or AR77XB: Unconfirmed, TCNEW-T4.

6. With the belt slipped off the inner platter, spin the inner platter by hand. It should turn freely and coast. If it is stiff or will not turn, do not power the table: the bearing needs cleaning and oil, see the [platter bearing](../models/xa/mods/platter-bearing.md). Early XA bearings were cast Delrin, which closed in and seized the spindle (Owner report, [Allison](archive/vinyl-nirvana/ar-turntable-history/acoustic-research-inc-the-golden-years-by-roy-allison.md)). Vinyl Nirvana has found a seized platter bearing on every older XA it has seen with that material (Specialist, [photo comparison](archive/vinyl-nirvana/ar-turntable-history/a-photo-comparison-of-a-new-and-old-ar-xa.md)).
7. Refit the belt and the outer platter. Press the platter gently down at the edge and let go: it should bob and settle level. Measure the gap between the bottom of the outer platter and the top plate. Factory value with a heavy record on: XA ⅛ in (3.2 mm), XB ⅜ in ± ¹⁄₃₂ (9.5 ± 0.8 mm) (Factory, XA service manual, p. 2; [XB drawing 119](drawings/xb-drawing-119.png), note 13). If it rubs or tilts, see [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#platter-rubs-the-top-plate).
8. Lift the arm off its rest and move it across by hand. It should swing freely. On the XA it should sink slowly when lifted and released: that is the factory arm damping (Factory, [XA owner's manual, p. 7](transcripts/xa-owners-manual.md)).
9. Look at the mains cord and the signal cable along their whole length. One restorer found both "pretty frail" on a table that otherwise looked good (Owner report, [John's Weather 2009](archive/johns-weather/xa-restoration-2009.md)). Replace a cracked cord before use.
10. Look at the stylus under a magnifier. The same restorer found the stylus on the original Shure cartridge worn out (Owner report, same page). Do not play a record with a damaged stylus.
11. Only now plug it in and switch on, with the outer platter in place: without it the belt is likely to come off (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 11). Check speed with a [strobe disc](../parts/strobe-disc.md): AR's limit is ±21 dots per minute (Factory, XA service manual, p. 2).

Second family: no unpacking or first-use instruction is held. Unconfirmed.

## How to move it

**Never invert it, and never carry it by the platter or the arm.** The first is factory instruction; the second is our assessment.

XA and every first-family table, from the service manual's preparation for opening the table (Factory, XA service manual, p. 2):

1. Unplug the table.
2. Take off the record mat.
3. Lift off the outer platter.
4. Hold the arm down in its rest while you remove the shell; this protects the pivots inside the arm (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 3).
5. Slide off the counterweight after loosening its set screw. Keep the screw.
6. Tie the arm to its rest.
7. Carry the base level. If it must be set down other than flat, brace it on its side, never upside down.

XB pattern, in addition: refit the wing-nut transit bolt, which secures the suspension to the top plate. AR says it must be used whenever the table is shipped (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 3). The XB1 is packed with the tonearm tied down by a yellow pipe cleaner at the rest and a blue one at the pivot, and three foam shims under the inner platter (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 2).

Second family: no transport instruction is held. Vinyl Nirvana's restoration service rents original packing for shipping a table to it (Maker claim, [restoration services](archive/vinyl-nirvana/restoration-services.md)).

## What came in the box

No source counts which parts go missing most often. The lists below are what the factory packed, so you can see what your table lacks.

| Item | XA | XB1 | Where to find one |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cartridge | Not included: chosen and bought separately (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 1; Owner report, Allison) | May come prefitted, or not (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 4) | Any cartridge that suits the arm: [sheet L-1591](transcripts/ar-cartridge-mount-l-1591.md) |
| Cartridge screws, standoffs and spacers | A card of small parts; save the leftovers for a later cartridge (Factory, XA owner's manual, pp. 1, 3) | A small envelope of screws in the accessory tray (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 4) | [Cartridge screws, bushings and shims](../parts/cartridge-hardware.md) |
| Overhang template, "AIM TOWARD PIVOT" | Yes: overhang plate (Owner report, Allison); plastic guide (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 4) | Tonearm adjustment guide (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 3) | [Overhang template](../parts/overhang-template.md) |
| Needle force gauge | Yes, in its own box with instructions (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 5); $1.00 as a spare (p. 12) | Stylus force gauge (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 3); $1.25 as a spare (p. 13) | [Gauge instructions](transcripts/needle-force-gauge.md) |
| Headshell | Packed separate from the arm; plugs into it (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 3); $2.00 as a spare (p. 12) | Cartridge shell; $4.00 with hardware as a spare (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 13) | [Headshell](../parts/headshell.md) |
| Counterweight | Packed with the table; a spare set screw was in the hardware envelope (Factory, XA owner's manual, pp. 3, 5) | Tonearm counterweight in the tray; spare screw in the cartridge envelope (Factory, XB1 manual, pp. 3, 4) | [Counterweight](../parts/counterweight.md) |
| Mat | Turntable mat (Owner report, Allison); $2.00 as a spare (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 12) | Foam rubber record pad (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 2) | No current maker found. AR's foam mat absorbs dirt and can disintegrate; wash it in a dilute solution of mild soap and water, or replace it (Factory, XA service manual, p. 2) |
| Dust cover | Transparent cover (Owner report, Allison); $3.00 as a spare (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 12) | Transparent cover in a plastic bag (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 2) | [Dust cover](../models/xa/mods/dust-cover.md) and the [JnB Audio listing](archive/jnb-audio/ar-xa-dust-cover.md) |
| Oil | Squeeze bottle in the accessory tray (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 11) | Lubricating oil (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 3) | Any #10 machine oil without additives, such as Golden Shell 10W (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 11) |
| Screwdriver | A long thin screwdriver, in the manual's photograph (Factory, XA owner's manual, p. 1) | Screwdriver (Factory, XB1 manual, p. 3) | |
