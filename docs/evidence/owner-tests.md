# What owners have measured since

[The evidence](README.md) · [Factory](factory.md) · [Period tests](period-tests.md) · [Against the reference tables](compared.md) · [Where the AR is weaker](weaker.md) · [Bench method](bench-method.md)

Tier 3 of the evidence: measurements by owners, on their own tables, with their own instruments. Every value is an Owner report. The table's serial number is recorded where the owner gave it. The repository owner's bench results go here, in the [same format](#adding-a-result), once made.

## One owner's filmed tests of a stock XA, 2018

M. Zillch, *Audiophile Turntable TESTED: Acoustic Research AR-XA!*, published 2018-12-28, 51 minutes. A video cannot be stored; the [record page](../archive/youtube/zillch-2018-ar-xa-tested.md) keeps its address, author, date and the author's own time marks. The author describes the table as a stock, unmodified later one-motor XA with a new belt, grommets and mat; the belt is an aftermarket one and the pulley carries some oxide. Serial number not given.

| Test | Result on film | Time mark | How it was done |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tracking force, table level | 1.96 g | 04:57 | Digital stylus-force gauge |
| Tracking force, table on a tilted shelf | 1.954 g | 10:36 | Same gauge after tilting the table; the neutral-balance arm holds the force |
| Headshell mass | 6.978 g, with its wiring | 13:14 | Balance. Villchur's figure was 7 g: [factory page](factory.md#arm-and-shell) |
| Pivot height, warp wow | A 1 kHz test tone read 997.5 to 1002.5 Hz through an artificial warp on the XA, against up to 1005 Hz on the comparison arm | 19:51 | 1 kHz band of the CBS STR-100 test record with a warp added; frequency read while the stylus rides over it; the comparison arm is one the author prices at $28,000 |
| Speed | 33⅓ rpm; the reading placed 8th of 316 tables in the app's database | 17:15 | A phone app measuring platter speed, with the stylus in the groove so that its drag is included. The app and its database are not held: SR43 |
| Acoustic feedback | No howl with the table placed on top of a full-range speaker at maximum volume, and no audible distortion before howling | 32:07 | Listening. The same test Milder described in 1963: [period tests](period-tests.md#what-ar-quoted-from-the-press) |
| Room rumble | The floating subchassis removes it; the author gives the suspension bounce as about 4 Hz | 43:38 | Listening, with the author's warning that it needs large speakers or headphones to hear |
| Spindle diameter | 0.283 in | 40:35 | Measured; the author calls it a full-size broadcast spindle |
| Hammer on the top plate | No skip; a second test with a hammer driving a nail into wood on the table itself | 28:15 and 50:37 | The 1963 test, repeated |
| McProud test | Passed at 33 rpm with the stock arm and shell | 38:35 | The off-centre record test from Villchur's paper |

The figures are those the owner displays on the film; they have not been repeated on another table. The video description also summarises Oakley's 1967 paper on skating force, which it calls an AR paper, and quotes a May 2009 magazine line; the paper is [held](../originals/audio-1967-03-oakley-skating-force-pp40-42.pdf) and names no affiliation for its author, and the magazine line is not held: SR42.

## The same owner's blind listening poll, 2022

M. Zillch recorded a stock XA with a Shure M97xE and posted the file on AVS Forum beside a file of the same music recorded by Michael Fremer from a TechDAS Air Force Zero, for a blind poll. The [record page](../archive/youtube/zillch-2022-blind-test-xa-breakdown.md) of the author's video keeps his posted result: of 60 forum members, 35 chose the XA's file and 25 the TechDAS file. The forum thread itself refuses automated access and is not held: SR37. A listening poll is not a measurement; it is recorded here because it is the only blind comparison of an XA against a current reference table that has been found.
