Print this page and fill it in at the bench. Each line closes or adds evidence to one or more rows in [Things to confirm](../../TO-CONFIRM.md). Every value written here is an Owner report for this one table. Afterwards, copy each result into the Result column of its row, with the model, serial and date.

**Before you start:** unplug the table. Remove the mat, outer platter, shell and counterweight before bracing it on its side, and secure the arm to its rest. Do not turn it upside down (Factory, [XA service manual, p. 2](../../docs/originals/xa-service-manual.pdf)). Photograph each label and part before you remove it.
