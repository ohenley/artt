## Result

Same revision. Every page the scans show has the same wording as the PDF, including the same typing errors ("Removethe", "manulaal", "crip connectors", "UNSIDE DOWN"), the same serial range XA176235 thru XA204462 for the gray, blue, red, gray motor wires, and the same capacitor values. No text needs writing out: the PDF already carries it, and its text is on the [XA page](../../models/xa/README.md).

## What differs

No wording differences found. One spot to check: on service2.jpg, tone arm step 2 appears to read "vertical pibot screws", where the PDF text layer reads "pivot". The PDF text layer is machine-read, so this may be the same misprint corrected by the text layer, not a different printing.

## Illegible spots

None. The scans are legible at their saved size. The PDF is the better copy for every page it shares with the scans.
