## Text

**AR INC.**

**INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE OF AR NEEDLE FORCE GAUGE**

1. Place the needle force gauge on the record mat, and place the needle in the dimple of the raised section. The gauge should be perpendicular to the cartridge shell, as shown in the photograph.

*Figure: the clear plastic balance gauge on the record mat. A small pan with a round weight sits at its left end; the cartridge shell's stylus rests on the raised section at the right, with the shell at right angles to the gauge beam.*

2. Place weights on the opposite pan until the arm balances. Weights of ¼, ½, 1 and 2 grams are provided; if stylus force greater than 3¾ grams is being measured a U.S. penny will serve as an accurate 3-gram weight.

3. After balance has been achieved, gently move the arm *down* again to make sure that it is free. In the case of the AR arm it is especially important to make sure that the pivot is in its released position while the stylus force is being measured. Damping release is accomplished simply by moving the arm as far down as it will go while on the gauge. If the gauge sticks in both extreme positions, a tight tone arm pivot is indicated.

ACOUSTIC RESEARCH, INC., 24 Thorndike St., Cambridge, Mass. 02141
