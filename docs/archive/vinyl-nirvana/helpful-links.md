### Helpful Links

[The Vinyl Engine’s AR Forum](http://www.vinylengine.com/phpBB2/viewforum.php?f=34)

If you have an AR turntable question, the answer is likely here.

[John Weather’s AR XA Rebuild](https://johnsweather.com/Turntable.html)

Great detailed pages

[Vinyl Nirvana eBay Store](http://stores.ebay.com/Vinyl-Nirvana?_rdc=1)

Never know what turntable or vinyl related product I might have listed!

[George Merrill’s Analog Emporium](http://www.gmanalog.com/)

George’s new (late 2007) website dedicated to Analog.

[Review of the AR XA](http://www.vintage-speaker-review.com/2013/11/acoustic-research-ar-xa-turntable-review.html)

Short review of the AR XA at Vintage Speaker Review

[Parts Express](http://www.partsexpress.com/)

Extensive parts and cleaning products for the do it yourselfer.

[AudioRoundtable](http://www.audioroundtable.com/)

Another helpful audio discussion forum.

[The Vinyl Engine](http://www.vinylengine.com/)

A wealth of info on turntables, tonearms, and all things in vinyl playback.

[AR History Thread at Steve Hoffman Site](http://www.stevehoffman.tv/forums/showthread.php?t=37924&highlight=acoustic+research)

More info on AR products, with a great timeline.

[AR EB-101 Reviews at AudioReview.com](http://www.audioreview.com/TurnTables/Acoustic%20Research/PRD_121295_1597crx.aspx)

Some knowledgable reviews of the venerable EB-101. Be sure to go to the second page of reviews.

[AR EB-101 raves at TNT-Audio](http://www.tnt-audio.com/guide/sources_e.html)

Some more praise of the venerable EB-101.

[Thread on Mods at 4music.net](http://www.laventure.net/tourist/vinylphiles.htm)

An interesting thread regarding mods to a Mapleknoll tt with many references to AR mods and George Merrill.

[Audio Century](http://www.positive-feedback.com/Issue15/audiocentury.htm)

Great article describing the impact of the AR XA turntable on the hi-fi world.

[Audio Asylum](http://www.audioasylum.com/index.html)

The archive in the Vinyl Asylum is a wonderful resource.

[Audiokarma](http://www.audiokarma.org/)

Their Turntable and Tapes forum is informative.
