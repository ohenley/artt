# Acoustic Research Drawings Archive - Audio Appraisal

> Archived copy, saved 2026-11-05. Original: <https://audioappraisal.com/acoustic-research-drawings-archive/>. Site: Audio Appraisal. Rights remain with the author and the original site.

These drawings were released by Acoustic Research into the public domain in 1990. An index of the archive was made available on the [Classic speaker Pages](http://www.classicspeakerpages.net) website in an Adobe flash format. The demise of flash meant the archive was impossible to access and though some work was done to index the drawings in a way that can be accessed in a modern browser, it is difficult to find.

Because of this, and concerns that the aforementioned website may not be as actively maintained as it once was, I am archiving the drawings here and making them available for historical and educational purposes. There are 492 drawings in total which I believe represents the complete archive, though if anyone wishes to contribute additional material please get in touch. I have also collected a large amount of documentation concerning Acoustic Research which I also intend to archive here in due course. These files are provided as-is and without any warranty. Likewise the table below, which is accurate to the best of my knowledge.

Use ctrl+f (or cmd+f on a Mac) to search for part numbers or model names. The drawing numbers directly link to the original TIFF image files. To save the files to your computer, right-click the links, or an open image, and choose ‘download file’, ‘save file’ or ‘save image’ depending on your browser.
