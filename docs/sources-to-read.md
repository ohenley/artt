## Not held: the site refuses automated access

Save each page from a browser, add it to the [archive](archive/README.md), then write out what it says.

- SR1: [Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/turntables/comments/1t5vqzh/ar_xa_tone_arm_counter_weight_tone_arm_rest/). The values are already on the [counterweight page](../parts/xa-counterweight.md) (CW1–CW10); the post itself is not held.
- SR2: [diyAudio thread](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/acoustic-research-ar77xb-search-for-new-headshell-bolts.407382/). The 14 shells, whether they are original, and posts 2 and 4 (HS1).
- SR3: Same thread. A reported 4-40, then M2.5, cartridge thread (HS1).
- SR4: [eBay headshell listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/144937941911) by redcedarprints. A printed XA shell design and its license.
- SR5: [AudioKarma AR77XB thread](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar77xb-questions.860919/). AR77XB details.
- SR6: [Vinyl Engine AR77-XB entry](https://www.vinylengine.com/library/acoustic-research/ar77-xb.shtml). An AR77XB manual (MD10).
- SR7: [Radiomuseum AR77-XB entry](https://radiomuseum.org/r/acoustic_ar77_xb.html). Overall size, about 16.4 × 5.5 × 15.6 in (DM3).
- SR13: [Vinyl Asylum](https://www.audioasylum.com/messages/vinyl/866913/psssst-hey-buddy-need-grado-cartridge-screws) (3-48), [Audiogon](https://forum.audiogon.com/discussions/grado-wood-bodies) (M2.6 or about 2 mm), and [a Reference Master thread](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/daveyw/grado-reference-master-cartridge-fixing-t184.html) (2.5 mm). The Grado wood-body screw thread (GR1).
- SR14: [Grado's Master4 page](https://gradolabs.com/products/master4). The body wood. The [retailer's listing](archive/sound-approach/grado-master4.md) says Australian Jarrah; American Osage was noted earlier from one of the two pages and needs checking on Grado's own.
- SR15: AudioKarma threads on the [XA grounding problem](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar-xa-grounding-problem.787681/) and [suspension spring upgrade](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar-xa-suspension-spring-upgrade.1102797/). Grounding (IE2) and springs (SK1).
- SR17: Stereophile, "A Glorious Time: AR's Edgar Villchur and Roy Allison", [Allison part 1](https://stereophile.com/content/glorious-time-ars-edgar-villchur-and-roy-allison-allison-part-1) and [part 2](https://stereophile.com/content/glorious-time-ars-edgar-villchur-and-roy-allison-allison-part-2). Allison's and Abe Hoffman's titles and dates, and anything on the turntable (MD8).
- SR18: John Atkinson, ["State of the AR-T"](https://hifinews.com/content/state-ar-t), *Hi-Fi News*. Ron Fone, why the first table was dropped, and the 1983 relaunch (MD8).
- SR19: AudioKarma threads on the [XA and XA91](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/whats-the-difference-between-an-ar-xa-and-xa-91.460741/) and on the [differences between the XA, XB, and The AR Turntable](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/differences-between-ar-xa-xb-and-the-ar-turntable.538145/). The TA, the XA91, and a serial-number study (MD3).
- SR22: [HiFi Shark](https://www.hifishark.com/model/acoustic-research-the-ar-turntable) and [Collectors Weekly](https://www.collectorsweekly.com/records/turntables). The Legend as the European name, and the XB "in two versions" (MD2).
- SR23: AudioKarma threads on an [XA overhaul](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar-xa-overhaul-time.1112265/), [XA pivot bearing parts](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar-xa-pivot-bearing-parts.1053357/) (VP1), and [Marc Morin's XA tips](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar-xa-other-marc-morin-tips-hints-instruction.967048/).
- SR24: diyAudio threads on [grey grease in an XB main bearing](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/acoustic-research-arxb-main-bearing-has-alot-of-grey-grease-in-there-i-thought-it-was-supposed-to-be-thin-oil.409524/latest) (PB1) and an [XA with custom mods](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/ar-xa-w-custom-mods.368650/).
- SR25: [Vinyl Engine's Acoustic Research library](https://www.vinylengine.com/library/acoustic-research.shtml). Which AR turntable manuals exist.
- SR26: [Schurter inlet datasheet](https://datasheets.globalspec.com/ds/Schurter/kd/f2b002c3-9424-4f27-9f5d-cd0383e9044e). IEC inlet cutout and fuse (PL40 to PL45, IE1).
