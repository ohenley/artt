# Sources to read

[Home](../README.md) · [Document index](README.md) · [Things to confirm](../TO-CONFIRM.md)

- SR1: [Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/turntables/comments/1t5vqzh/ar_xa_tone_arm_counter_weight_tone_arm_rest/). The author, the counterweight and rest values (CW1–CW10), and the owner's own XA as the source.
- SR2: [diyAudio thread](https://www.diyaudio.com/community/threads/acoustic-research-ar77xb-search-for-new-headshell-bolts.407382/). The 14 shells, whether they are original, and posts 2 and 4 (HS1).
- SR3: Same thread. A reported 4-40, then M2.5, cartridge thread (HS1).
- SR4: [eBay headshell listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/144937941911) by redcedarprints. A printed XA shell design and its license.
- SR5: [Audiokarma AR77XB thread](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar77xb-questions.860919/). AR77XB details.
- SR6: [Vinyl Engine AR77-XB entry](https://www.vinylengine.com/library/acoustic-research/ar77-xb.shtml). An AR77XB manual.
- SR7: [Radiomuseum AR77-XB entry](https://radiomuseum.org/r/acoustic_ar77_xb.html). Overall size, about 16.4 × 5.5 × 15.6 in (DM3).
- SR8: [XB1 manual](originals/xb1-owners.pdf). Full extraction of the 16 pages.
- SR9: [XA owner's manual scans](https://vinylnirvana.com/ar-turntable-resources/ar-turntable-history/ar-xa-manual). Full extraction.
- SR10: [1969 service manual scans](https://vinylnirvana.com/ar-turntable-resources/ar-turntable-history/1969-ar-xa-service-manual). Whether it is the same revision as the local PDF.
- SR11: [XA technical specifications](https://vinylnirvana.com/ar-turntable-resources/ar-turntable-history/1969-ar-xa-technical-specifications). The performance figures.
- SR12: [Audio Appraisal](https://audioappraisal.com/acoustic-research-drawings-archive/). AR's 1990 public-domain release of the drawings.
- SR13: [Vinyl Asylum](https://www.audioasylum.com/messages/vinyl/866913/psssst-hey-buddy-need-grado-cartridge-screws) (3-48), [Audiogon](https://forum.audiogon.com/discussions/grado-wood-bodies) (M2.6 or about 2 mm), and [a Reference Master thread](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/daveyw/grado-reference-master-cartridge-fixing-t184.html) (2.5 mm). The Grado wood-body screw thread (GR1).
- SR14: [Grado](https://gradolabs.com/products/master4) and [one retailer](https://soundapproach.com/grado-master4-timbre-series-turntable-phono-cartridge.html). The Master4 body wood, American Osage or Australian Jarrah.
- SR15: AudioKarma threads on the [XA grounding problem](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar-xa-grounding-problem.787681/) and [suspension spring upgrade](https://audiokarma.org/forums/threads/ar-xa-suspension-spring-upgrade.1102797/). Grounding (IE2) and springs (SK1).
- SR16: [Galo's article](https://audioXpress.com/assets/upload/files/galo2931.pdf). Its date: footers say 2008, the last says June 2005, and a mirror labels it July 2008.
