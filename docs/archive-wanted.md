# Pages to save

[Home](../README.md) · [Archived articles](archive/README.md) · [Sources to read](sources-to-read.md)

Web pages about AR turntables worth keeping before they disappear. None of them is held here yet: most refuse automated access and must be saved from a browser. To save one, follow the steps in the [archive index](archive/README.md), then change "Archived here" to Yes and tick it off in [sources to read](sources-to-read.md).

Addresses are written out but not linked: this reference links only to its own copies. The Wayback Machine column is from its availability service on 2026-11-18. "No snapshot found" means the service returned none; the page may still be saved there under another address. Anyone can save a page there from a browser.

Seeded from "Not archived" in the [archive index](archive/README.md#not-archived) and the "Not held" entries in [sources to read](sources-to-read.md).
