# AR77XB service

[Home](../../README.md) · [AR77XB](README.md) · [XB service](../xb/service.md) · [Things to confirm](../../TO-CONFIRM.md) · [Documents](../../docs/README.md)

Routine service for the AR77XB: oiling the two bearings, the motor, the cueing lift, the belt and pulley, and cleaning the cover, the top plate and the platter.

No factory document for the AR77XB has been found, [MD10](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#models-and-dates). Every factory value below belongs to the XB, the nearest documented table, and is shown for comparison only. Check each one on your own table and record the result in [Things to confirm](../../TO-CONFIRM.md).

The AR77XB has the same cueing lever and the same oil bearings as the XB pattern (Specialist, [Vinyl Nirvana XB77](../../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/ar-models/ar-xb77.md): "12″ S-Shaped tonearm in oil bearing with cueing" and a platter "in oil bearing").

## Before you start

- **No AR77XB value is factory-confirmed.** The XB values below are a starting point. If your table behaves differently, record it; do not force it to the XB figure.
- **Do not turn the table upside down.** The XA service manual warns against it (Factory, for comparison: [XA service](../xa/service.md#before-you-start)).
- **Turn the cue cylinder cap no more than ½ turn at a time.** That is the XB rule (Factory, XB1 manual p. 11, for comparison). Whether the AR77XB has the same cap: Unconfirmed, TCNEW-S5.
- **Oil on the belt stops clean starts.** Keep oil off the belt, pulley and inner platter rim.
- **The counterweight is aluminium, lighter than the XB's** (Specialist, Vinyl Nirvana XB77). Do not swap weights between tables while servicing; a swapped weight changes the balance.

## Yearly oiling

The XB procedure, for comparison: [XB service, yearly oiling](../xb/service.md#yearly-oiling). In short: lift the arm assembly about ½ in (13 mm) and put a few drops on the arm spindle where it enters the well; lift the inner platter straight up about 2 in (51 mm) and put a few drops on the platter spindle; keep oil off the belt (Factory, XB1 manual p. 12). Oil named for the XB: any #10 machine oil without additives, such as Golden Shell 10W (same page).

What oil and how much the AR77XB needs: not stated in any held source. Use the XB value, then record what you used.

Deep clean: the [platter bearing page](../xa/mods/platter-bearing.md). Ball and spacer sizes on the AR77XB: not recorded.

## Motor

Motor maker, label and capacitors: Unconfirmed, [EL5](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#motor-electrical-and-overall-size). Write down the motor label before touching it.

No held source says to oil the motor, or where. Unconfirmed, TCNEW-S1.

For comparison only: XA slow start or chattering is first cured by a new belt and a clean pulley (Factory, [XA service](../xa/service.md#motor)). The XB speed test is ±21 dots per minute at 1.5 g stylus force (Factory, drawing 119).

## Cueing lift

XB factory values, for comparison (Factory, drawing 119 notes 7, 8 and 13; XB1 manual p. 11):

| Setting | XB value |
| --- | --- |
| Lift height, with mat and one record | ¼ to ⁷⁄₁₆ in (6.4 to 11.1 mm) above the record |
| Lowered | At least ¼ in (6.4 mm) of drop below the record surface |
| Descent | 1 to 3 seconds from full height to the record |
| Spindle and cueing cylinder to top plate hole | 0.050 in (1.27 mm) minimum |
| Damping medium | Not named: Unconfirmed, TCNEW-S3 |

AR77XB values: Unconfirmed, TCNEW-S5.

### The lift does not come down, or comes down too slowly

Follow the [XB procedure](../xb/service.md#the-lift-does-not-come-down-or-comes-down-too-slowly), with these changes:

1. Before turning anything, look under the pivot assembly for a cue cylinder cap, and at the top of the cue plunger for a knurled cap. Photograph both. If either is missing or looks different from the XB description, stop and record it: the XB steps may not apply.
2. Time the descent before any adjustment, and write it down with your serial. That is the first AR77XB figure.
3. Make each adjustment in the XB steps, ⅛ turn for the knurled cap, no more than ½ turn for the cylinder cap.
4. Time the descent again and record the result beside the first.

## Pivot set screws

XB values, for comparison: tighten until friction is barely perceptible, then back one screw out ⅛ to ¼ turn (Factory, drawing 119, note 9). Too tight: the arm is sluggish and the force gauge seems to stick. Too loose: the arm assembly wobbles when the pivot assembly is rocked (Factory, XB1 manual p. 11).

## Belt and pulley

Steps: [belt and pulley page](../xa/mods/belt-and-pulley.md). Vinyl Nirvana's replacement belt names the XB77; it is not for 50 Hz (Maker claim, [belt listing](../../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/products/replacement-ar-turntable-belt.md)).

For comparison, the XB factory method: clean the belt with methyl alcohol, not rubbing alcohol; wipe the pulley and inner platter rim with the same; dust the belt with talcum powder; once a year (Factory, XB1 manual p. 12). The other sources' solvents and talc are in the [XB service page](../xb/service.md#belt-and-pulley).

## Cleaning without damage

- **Dust cover:** the XB1 method, for comparison: dry cloth, then a combination cleaner and wax; for heavy dirt, lukewarm (not hot) water with mild dishwashing detergent, a cold rinse, dry, then wax (Factory, XB1 manual p. 12). The AR77XB cover carries a printed metal square with the newer AR logo (Specialist); whether that square survives washing: Unconfirmed, our assessment is to keep it dry.
- **Top plate paint:** textured black (Specialist). No held source gives a safe cleaner. Unconfirmed, TCNEW-S2.
- **Base:** chipboard wrapped in walnut-look vinyl (Specialist). A cleaner for the vinyl wrap: Unconfirmed, TCNEW-S2.
- **Platter and mat:** for comparison, the XA service manual says to wash the foam mat in a dilute solution of mild soap and water, or replace it (Factory, [XA service](../xa/service.md#platter-and-mat)). The AR77XB mat: not described in a held source.
