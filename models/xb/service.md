# XB service

[Home](../../README.md) · [XB](README.md) · [XA service](../xa/service.md) · [Things to confirm](../../TO-CONFIRM.md) · [Documents](../../docs/README.md)

Routine service for the XB: oiling the two bearings, the motor, the cueing lift, the belt and pulley, and cleaning the cover, the top plate and the platter. Each value names its source.

Factory sources: [drawing 119](../../docs/drawings/xb-drawing-119.png), AR drawing 100012 revision T, assembly notes 7 to 14; and the [XB1 manual](../../docs/transcripts/xb1-owners-manual.md), form 700039, dated 3/76. The XB1 is the British 220 to 240 V XB, covered on the [XB page](README.md); its manual is the only factory operating manual for the XB pattern.

The XB has no arm damping. The cueing lift replaces the XA's damped arm (Specialist, [XB page](README.md#how-to-recognise-one)).

## Before you start

- **Do not turn the table upside down.** The XA service manual warns against it (Factory, for comparison: [XA service](../xa/service.md#before-you-start)). For the XB itself no factory paper says so. Galo warns that oil spills from the spindle well (Owner report).
- **Oil on the belt stops clean starts.** Without talc on the belt the table will not start properly (Factory, XB1 manual p. 12).
- **Do not pull the arm wire when moving the arm tube.** Pulling hard jerks the arm out of its mounting and breaks the arm wires (Factory, XB1 manual p. 10).
- **Turn the cue cylinder cap no more than ½ turn at a time** (Factory, XB1 manual p. 11).
- **Do not adjust the pivot set screws without a reason.** AR set and secured them at the factory and told owners to ask the factory or a service station before touching them (Factory, XB1 manual p. 11).

## Yearly oiling

Factory, XB1 manual p. 12. Oil: the squeeze bottle packed with the table, or any #10 machine oil without additives, such as Golden Shell 10W. The factory oil is Shell oil 10W, part 600013, item 35 on drawing 119.

1. Lift off the mat and the outer platter.
2. Arm spindle: lift the tone arm assembly about ½ in (13 mm).
3. Put a few drops of oil on the top of the spindle where it enters the well. Right: the oil runs into the well. Wrong: oil on the top plate; wipe it off.
4. Lower the arm assembly.
5. Platter spindle: lift the inner platter straight up about 2 in (51 mm). It does not need to come out of the bearing.
6. Put a few drops of oil on the spindle.
7. Lower the inner platter and turn it by hand. Right: it turns freely and the belt is still on its pulley step.
8. Check the belt and the inner platter rim for oil. If any, clean them as in [Belt and pulley](#belt-and-pulley).

At the factory: 3 drops on the arm and platter spindle bearings at final assembly, after cleaning the bearings (Factory, drawing 119, note 11). Note 11 cites the oil as item 36; the list gives oil as item 35.

## Deep clean of the bearings

The step-by-step clean is on the [platter bearing page](../xa/mods/platter-bearing.md), which uses the XB's drawing 119 notes. After a clean, drawing 119 note 14 runs the motor and platter for 24 to 48 hours before the final test (Factory). The XB parts: bearing ball 500013, bearing spacer 400061, two of them (Factory, drawing 119). Ball diameter: Unconfirmed, [PL6](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#platter-bearings-and-suspension).

## Motor

No held source says to oil the XB motor, or where. Motor lubrication: Unconfirmed, [EL9](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#motor-electrical-and-overall-size). The XB motor is part 802006, maker not stated (Factory, drawing 119).

The belt runs centred on the pulley; drawing 119 note 12 sets this with shims under the motor (Factory). Speed must hold ±7 dots in 20 seconds, or ±21 dots per minute, at 1.5 g stylus force (Factory, drawing 119, performance test A).

For comparison only: on the XA, slow start or chattering is cured first by a new belt and a clean pulley, and only then is the motor suspected (Factory, [XA service](../xa/service.md#motor)).

## Cueing lift

Factory values, drawing 119 notes 7 and 8, and XB1 manual pp. 8 and 11:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Lift height, with mat and one record | Raises the stylus ¼ to ⁷⁄₁₆ in (6.4 to 11.1 mm) above the record |
| Lowered | Leaves at least ¼ in (6.4 mm) of drop below the record surface |
| Descent | 1 to 3 seconds from full height to the record |
| Clearance, arm spindle and cueing cylinder to the top plate hole | 0.050 in (1.27 mm) minimum (note 13) |
| Damping | "Viscous damped" (XB1 manual p. 8). The damping medium is not named: Unconfirmed, [CU3](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#cueing-lift-and-arm-damping) |

The cue cylinder cap is under the pivot assembly and sets the descent. The knurled cap on top of the cue plunger sets the height (Factory, XB1 manual p. 11, Figure 12).

### The lift does not come down, or comes down too slowly

1. Move the cue lever to the rear, the lowered position, with a record on the platter. Watch the stylus.
2. If the stylus stays above the record, check the lift height first. Turn the knurled cap at the top of the cue plunger clockwise about ⅛ turn. Clockwise lowers it (XB1 manual p. 11). Our assessment: a lift set too high can hold the stylus off the record even with the lever back.
3. Lower the lever again. Repeat step 2, ⅛ turn at a time, until the stylus reaches the record and the lowered lift sits at least ¼ in (6.4 mm) below the record surface (note 7). Wrong: the lift now rises less than ¼ in (6.4 mm) above the record when the lever is forward; turn back counterclockwise.
4. Time the descent: lever forward, wait for full height, lever back, count the seconds to the record.
5. If it takes more than 3 seconds, turn the cue cylinder cap clockwise, no more than ½ turn (XB1 manual p. 11). Clockwise makes the arm come down faster.
6. Time it again. Repeat step 5 until it takes 1 to 3 seconds.
7. If it comes down in under 1 second, turn the cap counterclockwise, ½ turn at a time.
8. Look at the arm spindle and the cueing cylinder where they pass through the top plate. They must not touch the edge of the hole: at least 0.050 in (1.27 mm) of clearance (note 13). Our assessment: a part touching the plate can hold the lift up; the [platter height gauge](../../parts/platter-height-gauge.md) page covers the same clearance.
9. If the descent cannot be brought into range with the cap, the damping medium may have thickened or leaked. How to open and refill the cylinder: no held source. Unconfirmed, [CU3](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#cueing-lift-and-arm-damping).

The cueing lift can also be used as a pause during play (XB1 manual p. 8).

## Pivot set screws

- Too tight: the arm is a little sluggish, and the force gauge acts as though it is sticking (Factory, XB1 manual p. 11).
- Too loose: rocking the pivot assembly from side to side makes the aluminium arm assembly wobble too much (same).
- Factory setting: tighten until friction is barely perceptible, then back one screw out ⅛ to ¼ turn (Factory, drawing 119, note 9).

## Belt and pulley

The cleaning steps are on the [belt and pulley page](../xa/mods/belt-and-pulley.md). Factory method for the XB pattern, XB1 manual p. 12:

1. Take the belt off.
2. Clean it with tissue soaked in methyl alcohol (wood alcohol). Do not use rubbing alcohol.
3. Wipe the pulley and the inner platter rim where the belt rides, with the same alcohol.
4. Dust the belt with ordinary talcum powder.
5. Refit it. The larger pulley flange is 45 rpm; tables leave the factory set for 33⅓ (XB1 manual p. 7).

Clean and powder the belt once a year (same page). Other sources differ: Vinyl Nirvana uses 99.5% isopropyl alcohol and a green scrub pad (Owner report), and Galo says pure talc, not talcum powder (Owner report). They stay side by side.

## Cleaning without damage

### Dust cover

Factory, XB1 manual p. 12:

1. Wipe it with a dry dust cloth whenever you dust the furniture.
2. When fingerprints no longer wipe off, or grime has built up, apply a good combination cleaner and wax, and wipe dry as its directions say.
3. For heavy dirt, wash the cover in lukewarm water, not hot, with mild dishwashing detergent.
4. Rinse it in clear cold water.
5. Wipe it dry.
6. Wax it as in step 2.

### Top plate paint

No held source gives a safe cleaner for the black XB top plate. Unconfirmed, [CL1](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#other-parts). On an XA, paint stripper lifted the top plate finish within ten minutes (Owner report, [XA service](../xa/service.md#top-plate-paint)), for comparison.

### Platter and mat

The XA service manual says to wash the foam mat in a dilute solution of mild soap and water, or replace it (Factory, for comparison: [XA service](../xa/service.md#platter-and-mat)). The XB mat: not described in a held source. A cleaner for the platter surface that does not mark the aluminium: Unconfirmed, [CL1](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#other-parts).
