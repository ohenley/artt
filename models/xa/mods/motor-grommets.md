# Motor mounting grommets

[XA](../README.md) · [Mods](README.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Reduce motor vibration reaching the top plate by mounting the motor on soft grommets. Unproven: no measured comparison exists. The XB mounts its motor the same way, with item 18.

## Stock situation

Two screws hold the motor to the top plate, with no motor grommet in the parts list: see the [XA service manual](../../../docs/originals/xa-service-manual.pdf) and [Motor identification](../README.md#motor-identification).

## Parts

| Part | What is established |
| --- | --- |
| Grommets | No specific part found. Size and hardness TBC |
| Motor screws | XB: #4-48 × ⅛ in, item 18. XA: TBC. Grommets may need longer screws |

## Procedure

1. Record speed and belt position first.
2. Unplug the table, then remove the two motor screws.
3. Fit the grommets, keeping the motor's height and tilt as close to stock as you can.
4. Refit the screws without crushing the grommets.
5. Repeat the speed and belt checks.

## Gotchas

- **The motor's tilt sets speed and belt position.** The XA manual varies speed with a shim under one end of the motor. XB note 12 centers the belt with shims. Soft mounts can change both.
- **The motor is already on the isolated side.** It sits on the top plate, away from the sprung subchassis, and the belt couples them. Measure before assuming a gain.
- **The grommet in the top plate is not for the motor.** The manual's rubber grommet is for the arm wires.

## Verification

1. Check speed with a strobe disc. The XA manual allows no more than ±21 dots per minute.
2. The belt stays centered on the pulley.
3. Compare vibration on the top plate by touch or with a phone accelerometer, using the same method before and after.
