# Motor mounting grommets

[XA](../README.md) · [Mods](README.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Reduce motor vibration reaching the top plate by mounting the motor on soft grommets. **This is unproven.** No measured comparison was found.

The XB mounts its motor the same way, with item 18, so the same questions apply. Nothing is confirmed for the AR77XB.

## Stock situation

See [Motor identification](../README.md#motor-identification). The [XA service manual](../../../docs/originals/xa-service-manual.pdf) describes two screws holding the motor to the top plate. Its parts list shows no motor grommet. The rubber grommet it describes in the top plate is for the arm wires.

## Parts

| Part | What is established |
| --- | --- |
| Grommets | No specific part found. Size and hardness to confirm on the part |
| Motor screws | XB: #4-48 × ⅛ in, item 18. XA: to confirm on the part. Grommets may need longer screws |

## Procedure

1. Record speed and belt position first.
2. Unplug the table, then remove the two motor screws.
3. Fit the grommets, keeping the motor's height and tilt as close to stock as you can.
4. Refit the screws without crushing the grommets.
5. Repeat the speed and belt checks.

## Gotchas

- **The motor's tilt sets speed and belt position.** The XA manual varies speed with a shim under one end of the motor. XB note 12 centers the belt with shims. Soft mounts can change both.
- **Others went the opposite way.** A forum excerpt describes one of Marc Morin's mods as clamping the motor to the top plate, not soft-mounting it.
- **The motor is already on the isolated side.** The motor sits on the top plate, away from the sprung subchassis, and the belt couples them. Measure before assuming a gain.

## Verification

1. Check speed with a strobe disc. The XA manual allows no more than ±21 dots per minute.
2. The belt stays centered on the pulley.
3. Compare vibration on the top plate by touch or with a phone accelerometer, using the same method before and after.
