# XA mods

[XA](../README.md) · [Parts and makers](../../../parts/README.md) · [Things to confirm](../../../TO-CONFIRM.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

Upgrades for anyone restoring an XA. Each page says if a mod also applies to the XB or later models. **None of these has been built or tested for this repository.** Values come from a cited source or are marked "to confirm on the part", and open items are in the [confirmation list](../../../TO-CONFIRM.md#xa-mods).

## Mods

- [RCA jacks in the plinth](rca-jacks.md): Stop a stiff interconnect from loading the subchassis.
- [IEC mains inlet in the plinth](iec-inlet.md): Fit a detachable, fused mains cord, with one star ground.
- [Tonearm rewire](tonearm-rewire.md): Replace the aged arm wire, and record the pin map.
- [Vertical pivot bearing upgrade](vertical-pivot-bearing.md): Use jewel cups for lower pivot friction.
- [Hardwood plinth](hardwood-plinth.md): Replace a damaged or low-grade base with solid hardwood.
- [Motor phase capacitor](motor-capacitor.md): Replace the aged motor capacitor with a new film part.
- [Headshell with top mounting holes](headshell-top-mount.md): Fit a Grado wood-body cartridge to the stock arm.
- [Tall acrylic dust cover](dust-cover.md): Give the arm more clearance under the cover.
- [Replacement armboard (T-bar)](armboard.md): Stiffen the link between the arm pivot and platter bearing.

## Companion mods

- [Suspension kit](suspension-kit.md): Replace tired springs and grommets.
- [Belt and machined pulley](belt-and-pulley.md): Restore speed accuracy and clean starts.
- [Platter bearing clean and oil](platter-bearing.md): Clean, inspect the thrust ball, and oil the bearing.
