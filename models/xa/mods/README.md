# XA mods

[XA](../README.md) · [Parts and makers](../../../parts/README.md) · [Things to confirm](../../../TO-CONFIRM.md#xa-mods) · [Home](../../../README.md)

Upgrades for an XA. Each page says if it also applies to the XB. **TBC** means to be confirmed on the part; each TBC is a row in the confirmation list.

## Mods

- [RCA jacks in the plinth](rca-jacks.md): stop a stiff interconnect from loading the subchassis.
- [IEC mains inlet in the plinth](iec-inlet.md): a detachable, fused mains cord, with one star ground.
- [Tonearm rewire](tonearm-rewire.md): replace the aged arm wire, and record the pin map.
- [Vertical pivot bearing upgrade](vertical-pivot-bearing.md): jewel cups for lower pivot friction.
- [Hardwood plinth](hardwood-plinth.md): replace a damaged or low-grade base.
- [Motor phase capacitor](motor-capacitor.md): replace the aged capacitor with a film part.
- [Headshell with top mounting holes](headshell-top-mount.md): fit a Grado wood-body cartridge.
- [Tall acrylic dust cover](dust-cover.md): more clearance over the arm.
- [Replacement armboard (T-bar)](armboard.md): stiffen the link between arm pivot and platter bearing.

## Companion mods

- [Suspension kit](suspension-kit.md): replace tired springs and grommets.
- [Belt and machined pulley](belt-and-pulley.md): restore speed accuracy and clean starts.
- [Platter bearing clean and oil](platter-bearing.md): clean, inspect the thrust ball, and oil.
- [Motor mounting grommets](motor-grommets.md): soft-mount the motor; unproven.
- [Constrained-layer damping under the top plate](top-plate-damping.md): reduce top plate ringing; unmeasured.
