# Constrained-layer damping under the top plate

[XA](../README.md) · [Mods](README.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Reduce ringing in the metal top plate. Unmeasured: only owner reports exist. The XB has a similar top plate, with different clearances.

## Stock situation

Top plate thickness and material are missing: see the [part index](../../../parts/reproduction.md#body-and-cover).

## Procedure

1. Remove the plate's parts, and clean its underside.
2. Tap the plate and listen, as a before reference.
3. Mark where everything moves or sits: arm spindle, bearing, motor, switch, capacitors, and wires.
4. Fit the damping sheet, keeping clear of those areas.
5. Tap again, then reassemble.

## Gotchas

- **Do not block the moving parts.** The subchassis must not touch the damping sheet at any point in its travel.
- **Keep the capacitor clearance.** On the XB, ECO 1315 keeps the motor capacitor at least ⅛ in from the top plate, for a UL requirement.
- **Damping the T-bar is a different job.** The T-bar is sprung, so added mass there changes the suspension. See [Replacement armboard](armboard.md).
- **Do not trap heat around the motor.** Leave its body clear.

## Verification

1. Tap the plate. Ringing should be shorter than before.
2. Press and release the platter. Nothing should touch.
3. Check the platter clearance: see [Service details](../README.md#service-details).
4. Confirm the capacitors and wires are clear.
