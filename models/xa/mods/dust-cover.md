# Tall acrylic dust cover

[XA](../README.md) · [Mods](README.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Give the arm more clearance under the cover, including a shell and cartridge that stand higher than stock. The listing names only the XA.

## Procedure

1. Measure the plinth's outside size, and the height of the arm and cartridge at rest.
2. Ask the maker for the cover's inside dimensions, and compare.
3. Fit the bumpers, and seat the cover on the corners.
4. Record its outside size, inside height, and clearance over the arm on this page.

## Gotchas

- **The cover is not a copy of the original.** The maker says it was changed from the OEM version.
- **A new plinth changes the fit.** Check this if you are also fitting a [hardwood plinth](hardwood-plinth.md).
- **A rewired or modified arm may stand higher.** Measure with your actual shell and cartridge.
- **Use mild soap and water only.** The maker warns against glass cleaners, ammonia, alcohol, and solvents.

## Verification

1. The cover seats on all four corners without rocking.
2. It clears the arm, shell, and cartridge at rest.
3. A record plays with the cover down and nothing touching.
