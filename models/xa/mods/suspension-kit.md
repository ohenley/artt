# Suspension kit

[XA](../README.md) · [Mods](README.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Replace tired springs and grommets, and get finer adjustment of the subchassis. The listed kit also names the XB and XB-77.

## Stock situation

Original spring rate and stud length are Unconfirmed: see [Service details](../README.md#service-details).

## Parts

| Kit | Contents (Maker claim) |
| --- | --- |
| Vinyl Nirvana upgrade kit, XA, XB, XB-77 | 3 springs, 3 rubber grommets, 3 stainless washers, 3 stainless 2 in studs, 3 nylon-insert wing long nuts |
| Vinyl Nirvana heavy duty kit | 3 stiffer springs and grommets, for substantially heavier platters or arms |

## Procedure

1. Remove the platter, belt, and T-bar, keeping the T-bar upright so no oil spills.
2. Fit the new studs, springs, and grommets.
3. Refit everything, with a heavy record in place.
4. Adjust the three nuts until the platter clearance is right: see [Service details](../README.md#service-details).

Vinyl Nirvana has an [install video](https://vinylnirvana.com/ar-turntable-resources/ar-diy-do-it-yourself/installing-a-spring-and-stud-set-on-ar-turntable), which shows a mid-80s AR.

## Gotchas

- **Do not use the heavy duty kit on a stock table.** The maker says those springs are much too stiff for the traditional AR suspension and platter.
- **Longer studs may not fit a stock plinth.** One builder set aside heavier springs and studs because they were too long for his.
- **Clearance differs by model.** The XA manual gives ⅛ in, and XB drawing 119 gives ⅜ in ± ¹⁄₃₂.
- **The kit's parts are not an original specification.**

## Verification

1. The platter sits level at the set clearance, at all points.
2. Press and release the platter: it should bounce straight, without twisting or touching.
3. The arm spindle clears the top plate hole.
