# Belt and machined pulley

[XA](../README.md) · [Mods](README.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Restore speed accuracy and clean starts with a new belt. A machined pulley is a further option for some motors. The listed belt also names the XB and XB77.

## Stock situation

Pulleys differ by motor and mains frequency: see [Motor identification](../README.md#motor-identification).

## Parts

## Procedure

Supplies: new belt, talc, 99.5% isopropyl alcohol, cotton swabs, a green scrub pad.

1. Read the name on the motor, usually on its underside.
2. Lift off the mat and the outer platter. The motor pulley and the inner platter are now in view.
3. Take off the old belt. If it has bonded to the inner platter, lift the inner platter out and scrape the residue off with a putty knife.
4. Pull the pulley off with a quick, straight, upward pull. The manual suggests prying gently with two large spoons.
5. Lift the inner platter out of the bearing well, and push a small piece of cloth into the well to keep dirt out of the bearing.
6. Scrub the pulley and the belt surface of the inner platter with the pad and alcohol until the metal is smooth and clean.
7. Take the cloth out, refit the inner platter, and press the pulley straight back on. A new pulley goes on the same way.
8. Shake the belt in a bag with about a teaspoon of talc, then shake off the excess away from the table.
9. Fit the belt around the inner platter and the pulley. The upper step of the pulley is 33⅓ rpm.
10. Run the table for a week before judging. Vinyl Nirvana says its belt takes about that long to wear in.

Steps 2 to 9 are Vinyl Nirvana's illustrated belt replacement for the XA and XB (Owner report); the photographs are in the stored copy listed under Sources.

## Gotchas

- **The machined pulley does not fit stock motors.** Original Haydon and Philips XAs cannot use it without a motor change.
- **Pulleys are not interchangeable.** Philips and Haydon bores differ, at ¹⁄₁₆ in and ⅛ in, and 50 Hz pulleys are larger.
- **Belts need talc.** Galo says to use pure talc, not talcum powder, if a new belt comes uncoated.
- **A tight belt pulls the suspension.** Vinyl Nirvana warns that an overly tight belt drags the subchassis toward the motor.
- **The old belt source may have changed.** Galo's supplier is from 2008.

## Verification

1. Check speed with a strobe disc. The XA manual allows no more than ±21 dots per minute.
2. The belt runs centered on the pulley. XB note 12 sets this with shims under the motor.
3. The table starts cleanly, with no chatter.
