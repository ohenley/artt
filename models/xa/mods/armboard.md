# Replacement armboard (T-bar)

[XA](../README.md) · [Mods](README.md) · [Home](../../../README.md)

## Purpose

Stiffen the link between the arm pivot and the platter bearing. **Our assessment:** flex between those two points can be heard as smear and loss of bass. It is also the way to mount a different arm.

The XB uses the same kind of T-bar: item 26, part 404007, on [drawing 119](../../../docs/originals/xb-drawing-119.tif). Nothing is confirmed for the AR77XB.

## Stock situation

**The XA has no separate armboard.** The [T-bar](../../../parts/reproduction.md#platter-drive-and-suspension), item 14 in the [XA service manual, p. 4](../../../docs/originals/xa-service-manual.pdf), carries both the arm and the platter bearing on its three springs. "Armboard" on this page means a replacement for that T-bar. Its dimensions, thickness, and mass are to confirm on the part.

## Parts

| Option | What is established |

| Stock T-bar with added damping | One owner added rope caulk to a T-bar that rang when tapped |

| Replacement sub-chassis | Galo replaced his with Merrill Audio's acrylic sub-chassis. Current availability to confirm |

No replacement for the stock T-bar appears in Vinyl Nirvana's [AR parts list](https://vinylnirvana.com/product-category/ar-parts), checked 2026-11-04. Ask them directly.

## Procedure

1. Weigh the stock T-bar and record it.
2. Copy its spring positions, bearing center, and arm center. The arm-to-spindle distance sets the tracking geometry. See [Dimensions and arm geometry](../README.md#dimensions-and-arm-geometry).
3. Make or buy the new part, keeping its mass close to stock.
4. Refit the bearing, oil it, and refit the arm.
5. Level the suspension again, and retune the springs if needed.

## Gotchas

- **The board sits on the sprung subchassis.** Its mass shifts the suspension balance toward the arm corner. Keep it close to stock weight, or level and retune the springs again after fitting.
- **Stiffness is the goal, not weight.** Added mass changes the suspension.
- **Arm geometry is set here.** A wrong spindle-to-arm distance changes tracking. The Mayware builder found his arm's mounting distance slightly different from the AR arm, and his template was mispunched.
- **Keep the ground connection.** The manual ties the audio cable's ground strap to the suspension. The Mayware build needed a new ground lug on the T-bar.
- **Check clearances again.** On the XB, note 13 requires 0.050 in minimum around the arm spindle at the top plate.

## Verification

1. Weigh the new part, and compare with your stock figure.
2. The platter and arm sit level, with the platter clearance set: see [Service details](../README.md#service-details).
3. The arm spindle clears the top plate hole all the way around.
4. Press and release the platter. It should bounce straight, with no twist.
5. Check overhang with a gauge, and check for hum.
