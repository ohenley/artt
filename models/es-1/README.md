The Connoisseur Series table: shaped like The AR Turntable, with solid wood sides, a sapphire bearing and a Hurst motor, sold with a Japanese-made AR arm or without one.

**Not bench-verified. No table on hand.** No factory document for the ES-1 is held. Everything below is from specialist and owner accounts.

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Made | From 1984 (Specialist, VN ES-1). On sale by 1985 (Period, Wolf). "3 or 4 years after" November 1983 (Owner report, Dagogo comments). Conflict, [MD6](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#models-and-dates) |

## How to recognise one

| Look at | ES-1 | The AR Turntable, for comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sides | Solid wood, cherry or walnut, not veneered (Specialist, VN ES-1; Period, Wolf) | Walnut veneer all over |
| Edges | Rounded (Period, Wolf) | Rounded |
| Shape and size | Same essential shape and size as The AR Turntable (Specialist, VN AR the Turntable) | |
| Main bearing | Sapphire, with a loose ball under a concave platter tip (Specialist, VN ES-1) | Not stated |
| Motor | Hurst (Specialist, VN ES-1); see the conflict under Published figures | Early units not Hurst, per Vinyl Nirvana: [MD9](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#models-and-dates) |
| Arm | Often a different arm, since it was sold without one (Specialist, VN ES-1) | Usually the AR arm |

## Variants

- **With arm**: the AR arm, an OEM Japanese product that looks much like a Sumiko arm. It changed a little over the years (Specialist, VN ES-1).
- **Without arm**, for the buyer's own (Specialist, VN ES-1; Period, Wolf).
- **Cherry** or **walnut** sides (Specialist, VN ES-1).

Examples with other parts: a Sumiko MMT arm and aluminium armboard, bought about 1987 (Owner report, Dagogo comments); a Well Tempered arm on an acrylic board (Owner report, [Sadler ES-1](../../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/ar-gallery/sadler-es-1.md)); a Merrill subchassis, bearing, motor, power supply and springs with a Morch UP-4 arm (Owner report, [Carson's ES-1](../../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/ar-gallery/carsons-es-1.md)). A table like these no longer matches the recognition table above.

## Mechanical

- **Bearing:** sapphire, with a loose ball (Specialist, VN ES-1). Ball size and oil: Unconfirmed.
- **Subchassis:** Vinyl Nirvana says The AR Turntable and the EB-101 share the ES-1's subchassis (Specialist, VN AR the Turntable; VN EB-101). Its material is not stated.
- **Care:** one owner cleans and oils his ES-1 every winter and found that oil down the motor well cured motor noise (Owner report, Dagogo comments).
- **Shipping:** one ES-1 bought new in its box arrived with the arm still bolted down (Owner report, Dagogo comments). The transport bolt's position: Unconfirmed.
- **Belt:** Vinyl Nirvana sells a replacement belt listed for the ES-1, not for 50 Hz tables (Maker claim, [belt listing](../../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/products/replacement-ar-turntable-belt.md)).
- **Restoration:** Vinyl Nirvana offers restoration of the ES-1 (Maker claim, [restoration services](../../docs/archive/vinyl-nirvana/restoration-services.md)).

## Electrical

Motor, capacitors and mains wiring: Unconfirmed, [EL6](../../TO-CONFIRM.md#motor-electrical-and-overall-size). No ES-1 schematic is held. For comparison only, the factory Hurst schematics for The AR Turntable give 0.25 µF on the motor and 0.022 µF across the switch at 115 V, 60 Hz ([The AR Turntable, electrical](../the-ar-turntable/README.md#electrical)). Before changing anything, write down the motor label and both capacitor markings.
